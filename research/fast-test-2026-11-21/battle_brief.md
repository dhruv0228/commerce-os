## Who decides and what he wants

Dhruv Patel: software engineer in Florida, runs Meta ads for a client, strong at automation with Claude. 1 to 3 hours a day. He has never opened a Shopify store (can today). No TikTok Shop or TikTok ads account (can open in a day). He does not appear on camera.

His terms for this "fast test lane" (approved Nov 21, an exception for this test only):
- Cash: $500 to $1,000 all in.
- Speed: store, products and ads ready this week; first sales and clear data; a stretch goal of about $2,000 in sales by next week.
- Rule: every order should make money. He does not want to lose money per order.
- Sample: sell now, order one unit to himself the same day.
- Shipping: prefers US stock; international shipping is slow and has refund risk. Research decides.
- He does not want a market with a giant dominant seller, but wants proven demand.
- He wants a repeatable process he can automate, and a path to $10,000 a month profit.
- Barkfare (his dog back-seat extender kit brand) stays in the battle as the slow lane.

Fulfillment and money (03_fulfillment_and_money.md):
- No stock is bought up front, but each order is paid to the supplier before Shopify pays out. Shopify Payments: first payout about 7 to 14 days for a new store (ESTIMATED).
- Zendrop is not free once products are linked: $29/month for 1 to 20 products. US stock: 4 to 8 business days door to door. China: 10 to 15 days transit. No returns accepted on US items. VERIFIED vendor statements.
- TikTok Shop US: no international suppliers for US sellers, seller must own the inventory, dispatch in 2 days, 50 orders a day cap for new shops, payout 31 days after delivery. VERIFIED. Not a week-1 channel.
- Meta: a new ad account has a Meta-set daily spend limit; business verification can take up to 14 business days; about 50 purchases a week are needed to leave the learning phase. Client ad assets are not reusable.
- Black Friday week CPMs run 16 to 84% above September depending on the source. Christmas cut-off: US-stock orders by about Dec 10 to 14; China-shipped is effectively too late.
