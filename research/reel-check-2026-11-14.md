## Compliance notes

- **Skincare** brings FDA MoCRA duties for the brand on the label: safety substantiation, adverse-event reporting, label contact, and listing and registration unless exempt.
- **Claims:** marketing AI renders with "72%" ingredient claims risks FTC deception if the delivered product differs.
- **FTC Business Opportunity Rule:** "make $1,000 in one day" plus a $644 overlay is an implied earnings claim. If it's used to sell a program, the claim must be substantiated, with an earnings claim statement.
- **Shopify's dropshipping guide** puts typical net margins at 15 to 25% and recommends samples and supplier vetting. The Reel skips both.

## Takeaway

This is a lead funnel, not evidence that the method makes money. Everything we do stays grounded in a real sample, verified supplier costs, our own economics and real platform data.

The useful idea in it is the speed of AI-generated creative and quick ad tests. We can use that legitimately with our real product photos.
