## Positioning

Price and convenience: the same idea at a quarter of the premium price, backed by Amazon's returns. The bullets use bold Unicode letters. The brand story is "a decade of selling pet essentials in the U.S."

## Visible weaknesses

1. 6-month warranty.
2. Thin boards and sag in the cheap tier (category roundups and 4Knines' analysis, which says 8% of $50 to $100 tier reviews cite sag; ESTIMATED).
3. No real door-guard system beyond zip-on flaps.
4. Fit is left to the buyer's tape measure.

## What it means for us

- At $149 against $39.99, we must show what the extra money buys:
  - a rigid 3-panel board with a published sag test,
  - door guards,
  - a measured fit check,
  - a 1-year warranty,
  - US delivery in 2 to 5 days.
- Show it in ads side by side with a generic sagging board. Do not name URPOWER or use its images (CLAUDE.md section 7).
