## Visible weaknesses

1. The discount badge overstates the saving.
2. Two different warranty lengths (18 months and 2 years).
3. 14-day and 30-day return windows in the same policy.
4. No SD card in the base box.
5. A 166-product catalog, including rangefinders, refurbished units and fee SKUs, which dilutes the brand.
6. The "4K" footnote invites doubt.

## What we should learn

- **Adopt:**
  1. User-submitted incident footage as social proof and ad material, with permission.
  2. A short choice quiz.
  3. Bundle cards (camera, card, hardwire kit) on the PDP.
  4. Stated install help (video plus a guide).
  5. A visible US phone line.
- **Not for our budget:** a dealer network, many SKUs, or R&D claims.
