## Page structure

- **PDP:**
  1. Sticky sub-nav (overview, specs, compare, accessories, video, FAQs, review).
  2. Gallery with an image/video toggle.
  3. Title, review count and price.
  4. Ship, return and warranty box.
  5. Spec bullets.
  6. Optional accessories with checkboxes.
  7. Long feature sections.
  8. Wiring diagram.
  9. Product size.
- **Home:** Black Friday nav, "since 2010" hero, lineup tabs with sale cards.

## Visible weaknesses

1. Different prices on the homepage and the PDP.
2. A postal shipping policy of 8 to 12 business days with possible duties.
3. Only 14-day returns.
4. Few on-site reviews.
5. Copy errors: the A229 Plus page refers to the "A229 Pro".
6. Parking mode needs a hardwire kit sold separately.
7. Dense jargon (IMX675, HDR, bitrate) that loses first-time buyers.

## What we should learn

- **Adopt:**
  1. The accessory checkbox picker. It is a clean way to build a kit.
  2. A sticky sub-nav on long PDPs.
  3. The plain "ships within 2 business days" box near the price.
- **Our angle against VIOFO:** VIOFO is the enthusiast's choice. We should speak to first-time buyers in plain English, with the kit complete in the box.
