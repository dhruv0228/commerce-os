## Lessons for our brand

- **Product ladder:** one customer, a hero first, then add-ons, merch, bundles, and later a new hero.
- **An item people reorder:** for our dog-car customer, the analogue of the supplement tub could be paw wipes or an odor remover (enzyme cleaner and odor eliminator rows in data/opportunities.csv).
- **A credible face:** our options are a partner dog person or a brand dog.
