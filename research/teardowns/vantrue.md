## Visible weaknesses

1. Three different reference prices ($199.99, $299.99 and an auction from $90), with pre-order and in-stock signals mixed together.
2. An auction widget on a premium PDP.
3. Thin copy that is mostly a feature list, with typos ("GPPR", "Installtion").
4. No reviews on the hero.
5. The buyer pays return shipping.

## What we should learn

- **Adopt:**
  1. A bundle ladder: camera, then camera with card, then camera with card and hardwire kit.
  2. A privacy-mode feature message. Interior recording matters to rideshare drivers.
  3. Physical-button simplicity as a selling point for non-technical buyers.
- **Avoid:**
  1. Price games.
  2. Launching a hero with zero reviews and no plan to earn them.
