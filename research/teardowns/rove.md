## Visible weaknesses

1. **Delivery promise:** "1 to 3 days" in the banner, 5 to 7 business days in the policy, and no refunds for late shipments. Under the FTC Mail Order Rule a seller must have a reasonable basis for the time it states and must offer cancellation when it will be late.
2. **Returns:** the policy contradicts itself on who pays return shipping.
3. **Sensor:** the $119.99 R2-4K uses the first-generation IMX335, while REDTIGER and Vantrue use STARVIS 2 at a similar price.
4. **Box:** no SD card or hardwire kit in the base box, and parking mode needs the kit.
5. **Resolution:** the Dual's 4K front is labeled "2160P (Upscaled)".
6. **Copy and reviews:** typos ("Buit-In", "survivance") and no visible review count on the PDP.

## What we should learn

- **Adopt:**
  1. Honest bundle math with the card included.
  2. A family multi-pack for gifting (December peak, per `reports/holistic-analysis-2026-11-14.md`).
  3. A package-contents list near the top.
  4. A spec table that helps people choose.
  5. Open-box and refurbished SKUs later, to recover value from returns.
- **Avoid:** delivery promises the policy contradicts, and contradictory return terms.
