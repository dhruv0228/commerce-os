## Positioning and product strategy

- **Customer:** families with medium or large dogs and road trips. The pitch is calm dogs and clean cars.
- **Premium case:** the hard floor against a sagging hammock, a lifetime warranty, and a kit with freebies.
- **Upsell ladder:** bed, door guards, stairs, harness and leash, boots and coats. Higher order value comes from bundles and checkout add-ons (shipping protection, priority handling, extended warranty).

## Conversion design

The order timeline and the "what's included" block remove doubt. The price display relies on a large reference price and a countdown. Afterpay is there for a $160 to $200 basket.

## Visible weaknesses

1. **Reference price:** $363.99 is shown for a $159.95 product with a permanent "56%" badge. FTC 16 CFR 233 requires a former price to be a genuine one offered in good faith. Here that is unlikely, but a legal conclusion is not ours to make.
2. **Returns:** the no-returns-on-sale-items clause contradicts the 30-day money-back line.
3. **Reviews:** the count and rating differ across three places.
4. **Fit:** the dimensions conflict, and the "bridges bucket seats" claim clashes with Loox complaints of boards breaking across bucket seats (earlier research, `data/competitors.csv`).
5. **Extras:** it sells an extended warranty on a lifetime-warranty product, plus fees for shipping protection and "skip the line".

## Economics (public)

Kit $159.95 to $199.92. Margins, ad spend and volume are UNKNOWN. The long ad history (2,198 inactive ads) shows sustained creative testing, not profit.

## What we should learn

- **Adopt:**
  1. "The problem isn't your dog. It's the hammock." problem framing.
  2. A what's-in-the-box section with each freebie explained.
  3. An order-to-delivery timeline under add to cart.
  4. Free tether and bowl as low-cost value adds.
  5. The before/after asset.
- **Do not copy:** inflated reference prices, evergreen countdowns, checkout fee add-ons.
- **Not for our budget:** a broad accessory catalog, or influencer seeding at RuffRover's scale.
