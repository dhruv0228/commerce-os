## Short answer

1. Creator content is cheap to buy as ad material: about $89 to $259 per UGC video on fixed-price platforms (VERIFIED). But platforms add minimums or subscriptions (Billo $1,000 pack; Insense $400 to $650 a month plus a 7% to 20% fee; VERIFIED). The creator usually needs the physical product, shipped by us.
2. Paid posts on small pet creators' own accounts cost tens to low hundreds of dollars (ESTIMATED; Collabstr hides rates behind sign-up). Views per post vary by three orders of magnitude across creators of similar size (section 2). The click rate from an organic creator post to a store is UNKNOWN: no credible public benchmark was found. That one missing input decides whether a paid post beats Meta, so it cannot be claimed either way.
3. The best measured creator channel is TikTok Shop affiliate video, where checkout happens in the app. A median selling video sold 3.64 units per 1,000 views, but zero-sales videos were excluded and 86.8% of selling videos were also boosted with paid ads (ESTIMATED, Hubfluence, Nov 2026). So "free" creator sales on TikTok Shop are mostly paid-amplified.
4. Creator content run as ads (Meta partnership ads, TikTok Spark Ads) has the strongest evidence of beating normal ads, but the claimed gains are modest to medium and come from Meta, TikTok or vendors (section 6).
5. Pet is a weak category for video ad returns: in Billo's study of 80,069 Meta video ads, Pet Supplies had ROAS 1.66 against a 2.41 average and "finished last on both attention and sales" (ESTIMATED, vendor press release, May 29, 2026).

Does UGC beat other creative? Evidence:
- Billo says its creatives had CPA "11% lower, $82 vs $92" across "$1.24B" of brands' spend (ESTIMATED, vendor claim, search summary today, https://billo.app/billo-case-studies/).
- Billo H2 2025 benchmark, 80,069 Meta video ads, about $105 million spend, July to December 2025: average ROAS 2.41; Apparel 4.11; Home and Garden 3.43; Pet Supplies 1.66; "Animals & Pet Supplies finished last on both attention and sales" (ESTIMATED, vendor press release dated May 29, 2026, https://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/BILLO_Benchmarking_2026_05.pdf).
- MHI Media, 74 DTC brands: video and image cost per purchase overlap heavily (repo, `04_reality_and_benchmarks.md`).

Reading: UGC is a cost of making ads, not a customer channel by itself. It still has to be bought media (Meta or TikTok) to reach people, so its cost per customer is the ad platform's cost per purchase plus the video cost spread over the orders it drives.

Fit with house rules: UGC creators must film the real sample, which matches CLAUDE.md "Faces". Each video needs a kit shipped to the creator, so each creator costs one kit at landed cost plus shipping (UNKNOWN until the supplier quote; see `data/economics_inputs.csv`).

## 8. Disclosure, usage rights and the AI rule

- FTC (VERIFIED, https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking):
  - Disclose when a creator got free product, payment or an affiliate commission.
  - Effective wording: "Ad:", "#ad", "Advertisement", "Sponsored by XYZ", "Paid post by [brand]". "Gifted by XYZ" for product-only. "#ambassador", "partner", "#comped" and "Gifted" without a brand name are not clear enough.
  - In a video, the disclosure "has the best chance of being clear and conspicuous if it's included in the video itself"; the description alone is "not enough"; a disclosure only in comments is "easily avoidable".
  - A platform's built-in label is "no guarantee" that disclosure is adequate; the brand keeps responsibility.
  - Brands need "reasonable programs in place to train and monitor" creators; for ephemeral posts, "require that paid posts aren't made without you approving them in advance".
  - "You can't talk about your experience with a product if you haven't tried it." "If you've only tried a product once, you can't suggest you use it regularly."
- 16 CFR 255.0 (VERIFIED, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.0): an endorser "could be or appear to be an individual, group, or institution"; on interactive media a disclosure "should be unavoidable" and must appear in the same channel as the claim (visual claim, visual disclosure). Virtual influencers are not named in the text. Our reading that an AI avatar "appears to be an individual" and so is an endorser is ASSUMED.
- 16 CFR 465, Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule, effective Oct 21, 2024: bars reviews or testimonials that misrepresent they are by someone who does not exist, which FTC guidance applies to AI-generated reviews; also bars buying fake followers or views (ESTIMATED, law-firm summaries and FTC links in search today, e.g. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-465, https://whitecase.com/insight-our-thinking/no-fake-clout-ftc-issues-new-rule-crack-down-fake-consumer-reviews-and).
- House rule (CLAUDE.md "Faces" and section 9): no AI person claims to have bought or used the product. This matches the FTC text above. Dhruv's AI twin may speak as the founder; it may not describe personal use it never had.
- Usage rights differ by platform: soona "unlimited, worldwide license" (VERIFIED); Insense perpetual digital license, page conflicts on copyright (VERIFIED); Billo terms not on the pricing page (UNKNOWN); paid posts on a creator's own account include no ad usage by default, and whitelisting is priced on top (section 6, ESTIMATED).

## 10. Inputs for a cost-per-customer script

Formulas for the script (no numbers computed here):
- Paid post, own store: cost per customer = (post fee + seed kit landed cost + seed shipping) / (views x link click rate x store conversion rate).
- Gifting: (seed kit cost + seed shipping) / (post rate x views x click rate x conversion rate).
- Own-store affiliate: commission % x order value + platform fee (Collabs 2.9% of commission or UpPromote %) + seed cost / orders per affiliate.
- TikTok Shop affiliate: commission % x price + referral fee % x (price + customer shipping) + sample cost / units per creator, plus any GMV Max or Spark spend.
- Partnership or Spark ad: benchmark cost per purchase x new-store multiplier x (1 - CPA change) + (UGC or post fee + whitelisting fee) / orders over the ad's life.
- Clipping: (rate per 1,000 views x (1 + platform fee)) x views / buyers.

## 11. Case studies (small or pet brands)

I did not find a small pet brand that publishes creator fees, posts, views and resulting customers together. What exists:

1. Houndsy (dog feeder). One founder TikTok over 15 million views; about 70,000 emails; $160,000 Kickstarter "with no paid advertising"; $456,000 year 1; $1.7 million year 2; about $15 million over 4.5 years; startup cost under $20,000. No paid creators named. ESTIMATED, founder-reported, https://www.entrepreneur.com/side-hustle/viral-tiktok-co-workers-start-a-side-hustle-made-456k-year-1-15m-houndsy (Apr 8, 2026). Lesson: the founder's own demo video, not paid creators, did the work.
2. Bike Tow Leash (dog bike leash). $17,000 TV commercial aired 150 times sold 36 leashes; the founder turned down "5 figure" celebrity endorsement deals; free leashes to adoption facilities; reviews and videos from trainers and bloggers (count not given). ESTIMATED, founder-reported, https://starterstory.com/dog-bike-leash (2019). Lesson: famous-name endorsements cost five figures; paid reach can sell almost nothing.
3. PlayfulPup (dog toys, TikTok Shop, agency GR0). "+259%" revenue and "+320%" customers month over month; "10 million" organic views. No spend, price, creator count or commission disclosed. ESTIMATED, agency-reported, https://gr0.com/case-study/playfulpup.
4. Pet Republic (pet food, Poland, TikTok case). Spark Ads of its own organic posts: "over 9.3M impressions", "over 1.5 million users", "360% lower CPM" than another platform. No CPA or spend. ESTIMATED, platform case study, https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-GB/inspiration/pet-republic-tiktok-success-story.
5. The Farmer's Dog (large brand, for contrast). TikTok case: 62% lower CPA in two months, 33M views. ESTIMATED, platform case study, https://www.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/farmer's-dog-503 (search summary).
6. Billo pet category data (not one brand). Pet Supplies ROAS 1.66 against 2.41 average on Meta video ads, H2 2025. ESTIMATED, vendor.

## 12. Which products are better advertised by creators than by Meta ads

Plainly, from the evidence above:

- Better with creators: products under about $50 sold on TikTok Shop, where checkout is in the app, the creator is paid only by commission after a sale, and the product shows its result in the first seconds (a toy the dog attacks, a gadget with an instant before-and-after). This is where the measured creator data comes from (median GMV per unit $33.58). Even there, most selling videos were also boosted with ads, and sales are concentrated in a few videos.
- Also better with creators: products whose natural user is already on the creator's camera (a dog, a desk, a car interior) and that a creator would show without a script. Giftable, seasonal products do best near the gift window (Billo's toys data).
- Not better with creators alone: a $149 considered purchase sold on our own store. Paid posts give a few hundred to a few thousand views with an UNKNOWN click rate, each creator needs a full kit, and pet is the weakest video-ad category in Billo's data. For the Barkfare kit, creators are best used as the source of ad creative (UGC filmed with the real sample) and, once a post performs, as Meta partnership ads or Spark Ads, where evidence of lower cost per purchase exists but the gains are modest.
- TikTok Shop for the kit: only after a TikTok answer on imported inventory, with 2-day dispatch from Dhruv's home, and with cash able to wait about 31 days for settlement.

Recommended reading for the decision: cost per customer from creators cannot be shown to beat $39.23 (Pets Meta) until the click rate and post rate are measured. A cheap way to measure them: a small paid test with tracked links or codes (needs Dhruv's approval per CLAUDE.md section 10).
