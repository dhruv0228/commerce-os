# Low-cost ways to win customers: Barkfare, Black Friday, Christmas and after

Research date: Sunday 2026-11-22. Researcher: Claude (research step for the launch plan).
Scope: tactics other than paid Meta ads that a brand-new, one-product store can use. This builds on `research/fast-test-2026-11-21/03_fulfillment_and_money.md` section 6 (Meta CPM around BFCM and Christmas carrier cutoffs) and does not repeat it.

Labels: VERIFIED (read on a primary page today), ESTIMATED (third-party or self-reported, link and date given), ASSUMED (our placeholder), UNKNOWN (not found; what was tried is stated).
No arithmetic is done in this file. Section 5 lists the inputs a script should use.
Nothing was contacted, signed up for, logged into, posted, applied for or bought.

## 0. The findings that matter

1. **The house rules block any selling page before the sample arrives.** CLAUDE.md section 2: "A physical sample reviewed by Dhruv before any product page or ad goes live", with hero images from the real sample. CLAUDE.md section 0 says nothing has been bought under the fast test. So no tactic in this file can produce a Black Friday sale unless the 5-kit express batch arrives and is approved first. (VERIFIED, repo CLAUDE.md.)
2. **Google free listings cost $0 but review takes time.** Google: "Reviews typically take up to 3-5 business days for Shopping ads and free listings", and new accounts get an initial review. Thanksgiving falls in that window. (VERIFIED, https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/160491, read 2026-11-22.)
3. **Search clicks for our exact product are cheap compared with a Meta purchase, but the volume is small.** Google Keyword Planner data via DataForSEO, US: "dog back seat extender" about 590 searches a month, average CPC $1.98, top-of-page bid range $0.56 to $2.50. "dog christmas gifts" 5,400 searches, CPC $2.63, bid range $0.33 to $5.18. These are Search bids, not Shopping CPCs. A US Shopping CPC for pet supplies was not found (UNKNOWN). (ESTIMATED, DataForSEO, 2026-11-22; full table in 3.5.)
4. **Posting more short videos raises the odds of a breakout, not the typical result.** Buffer, 11.4 million TikTok posts from 150,000+ accounts: median views per post stay near 500 at every posting frequency (489 at one a week, 459 at 11+). The 90th percentile rises from 3,722 to 14,401. No data links those views to sales for a new store. (VERIFIED for Buffer's figures, https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/, published Oct 8, 2025, read 2026-11-22.)
5. **Meta bans "share or tag to enter" giveaways on Facebook Pages and groups.** Policy text: "Your promotion must not require or incentivize participants to share, repost, tag others, or in any other way publicize your promotion." Instagram requires a full release and a non-endorsement acknowledgement from every entrant. (VERIFIED, both read 2026-11-22.)
6. **Reddit's sitewide rule is short and strict in spirit.** Rule 2: "Participate authentically in communities where you have a personal interest, and do not spam." The "9:1" ratio is a community norm repeated by blogs, not a rule text we could read. (VERIFIED for Rule 2; the ratio is ESTIMATED.)
7. **Email tools are free at our scale.** Shopify Messaging: "Your first 10,000 emails each month are free." Klaviyo free plan: 250 active profiles and 500 emails a month, with $5 of mobile messages a month. (VERIFIED, app and pricing pages read 2026-11-22.) Email capture converts traffic we already have; it does not create traffic.
8. **No independent study shows gift-with-purchase beats percent-off for a new store.** One peer-reviewed paper (Chen et al., Journal of Marketing, 2012) found a bonus pack outsold an economically equal price discount in a retail field test, but that was extra quantity of the same item, not a separate gift. Vendor data is self-selected. For Barkfare, CLAUDE.md also forbids calling the door guards "free" until they have sold separately at that price.
9. **Etsy is closed to this product.** Etsy's help pages say "reselling mass-produced items is not allowed", with exceptions only for sourced craft supplies and vintage. (ESTIMATED; Etsy's own page returned 403 today, text taken from search snippets of help.etsy.com.)
10. **Amazon is the only marketplace that can open fast and has buyers already searching.** Individual plan $0.99 per item sold, Professional $39.99 a month (VERIFIED, https://sell.amazon.com/pricing). Pet referral fee about 15% (ESTIMATED). A new listing has no reviews against established ones. Walmart approval is reported at 1 to 4 weeks (ESTIMATED), so it misses Christmas.

## 3. Tactic details

### 3.1 Organic short video from a faceless account (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)

How it works: post short videos of the real kit in a real car (dog jumps in, platform holds, before and after), filmed by Dhruv's phone or a paid UGC creator, narrated by voiceover or Dhruv's AI twin as founder. No face needed. Link to the store in bio.

Evidence:

| Claim | Figure | Label and source |
|---|---|---|
| Views per post by weekly posting frequency, TikTok | Median: 489 (1 a week), 506 (2 to 5), 487 (6 to 10), 459 (11+). 90th percentile: 3,722, 6,983, 10,092, 14,401 | VERIFIED, Buffer, https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/ (Oct 8, 2025; 11.4M posts, 150,000+ accounts; fixed-effects regressions) |
| Mean lift in views per post versus once a week | +17% (2 to 5), +29% (6 to 10), +34% (11+) | VERIFIED, same |
| Buffer's reading | Median "actually fall[s] a bit at higher cadences"; gains come from more breakout posts; "returns are diminishing"; "quality will always be the most important factor" | VERIFIED, same |
| Instagram Trial Reels (shown only to non-followers) | Launched Dec 2024; reported available to public accounts with at least 1,000 followers; Instagram says users saw an 80% increase in Reels reach among non-followers | ESTIMATED, Meta newsroom https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first/ and secondary reports (via search, 2026-11-22). A new account will not meet 1,000 followers. |
| YouTube Shorts for a new store | No data found | UNKNOWN. Not searched in depth; no primary figure found. |
| Organic views to sales for a new store | No data found | UNKNOWN. Earlier sweep (02_youtube_sweep.md) found practitioners mostly use paid Meta; TikTok Shop showed up only in tutorials. |

Honest reading: Buffer's data says volume buys more lottery tickets, not a better typical post. The common advice to post many times a day is not supported by evidence about sales. For Barkfare, footage must be of the real sample (house rule), so posting cannot start before the kit arrives. Before that, only non-product content (dog car-travel tips) is possible, which builds an audience slowly. Labels: costs and times ASSUMED.

Rules to respect: AI voice may speak as the founder but must not claim to have used the product (CLAUDE.md). Label AI content where the platform requires it. TikTok Branded Content Policy governs paid creators (VERIFIED page exists, https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/bc-policy/en, read 2026-11-22).
