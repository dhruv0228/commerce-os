# Verifying that a Chinese (or US) supplier can deliver, and the red flags operators watch for

Research date: 2026-11-18. Scope: a solo founder placing a 5-unit express mini batch (under $500) of a rigid dog back-seat extender kit to Florida, then a 50 to 100 unit private-label order. Labels follow CLAUDE.md section 5: anything not from an official page or quote is ESTIMATED (third-party) or UNKNOWN. Many sources found are sourcing-agency or inspection-company blogs that sell the services they recommend; this is flagged where it matters.

Access notes: Reddit threads were not returned by search (only vendor blogs summarizing seller experiences). Alibaba's official Trade Assurance landing page was read; its Terms and Conditions and guide PDF were not opened. No official QIMA price list was found. No official carrier (DHL, FedEx, UPS) 2026 China-to-US brokerage fee schedule was found.

## 1. Alibaba Trade Assurance and Made-in-China Secured Trading: what they cover, disputes, fees, what voids protection

### Takeaway
Both programs only protect money paid through the platform's own checkout to the order-specific account; any off-platform payment (direct wire, WeChat, Western Union, a "new" bank account) is outside protection. Trade Assurance covers quality and late or non-shipment only against what is written in the on-platform order contract, the dispute window is about 30 days from delivery, and it is mediation, not a guaranteed refund. Made-in-China's buyer terms are far vaguer (no stated windows or late-shipment coverage).

### Cited Findings
**Alibaba Trade Assurance (official landing page, read 2026-11-18; page copyright 1999-2024, no policy effective date shown)**
- Covers refunds if an order "arrives with defects, incorrect items, damages, or other product-related issues", and if the order has not shipped or goes missing — [Alibaba Trade Assurance](https://tradeassurance.alibaba.com/)
- Late shipment: delivery by the scheduled date "or you receive delay compensation"; the page does not state the compensation amount or formula — [Alibaba Trade Assurance](https://tradeassurance.alibaba.com/)
- Funds are "held in escrow and released to the supplier after the product is received and confirmed" — [Alibaba Trade Assurance](https://tradeassurance.alibaba.com/)
- Eligible payment methods: credit/debit cards, digital wallets, bank and wire transfers "made through Alibaba.com"; the landing page does not explicitly list exclusions, fees or dispute deadlines (those are in the linked T&Cs, not read) — [Alibaba Trade Assurance](https://tradeassurance.alibaba.com/)
- "Easy Return" (free local return of defective goods) applies only in eligible countries; list not reproduced on the page — [Alibaba Trade Assurance](https://tradeassurance.alibaba.com/)

**Alibaba Trade Assurance (third-party guides, ESTIMATED)**
- Dispute window: 30 days from delivery for buyer Level 3 or lower, 60 days for Level 4 or 5; supplier has 5 days to respond, then the buyer can escalate to Alibaba's dispute team — [Jingsourcing, undated, "Copyright 2026"](https://jingsourcing.com/b-how-to-use-alibaba-trade-assurance/)
- "You have 30 days from delivery to open a dispute"; disputes without clearly specified quality standards in the contract are not covered; payments outside Alibaba (direct bank transfer, WeChat Pay) are not covered; customs delays, post-departure shipping damage and logistics-provider issues are not covered; Alibaba can rule full refund, partial refund or for the supplier — [Epic Sourcing, published 2026-04-24](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/24-04-how-to-use-alibaba-trade-assurance-a-buyers-safety-guide)
- Buyer payment fees: 2.99% for PayPal, card, Apple Pay, Google Pay; about USD 40 for T/T wire; suppliers pay their own 2% to 3% TA fee (capped roughly $100 to $350) and usually build it into price; Western Union discontinued for US buyers under $2,500 since 2023-06-30 — [Jingsourcing](https://jingsourcing.com/b-how-to-use-alibaba-trade-assurance/)
- Offline payment is not protected: "Any offline methods can't protect your payment"; pay only to the order-specific virtual (Citibank) account — [Jingsourcing](https://jingsourcing.com/b-how-to-use-alibaba-trade-assurance/)
- Mediation duration estimates conflict: 10 to 15 business days, 7 to 30 days, 15 to 30 days across vendor blogs; refund payout 3 to 15 business days after approval. Mostly from 19pine.ai, a dispute-service vendor — [19pine.ai](https://www.19pine.ai/blog/refund-after-alibaba-misrepresentation); a May 2026 complaint describes an agreed refund unpaid for over 3 months — [ComplaintsBoard](https://www.complaintsboard.com/alibaba-refund-agreed-but-not-paid-for-over-3-months-c2019176)
- A seller account: shipment arrived as a different product, Alibaba mediated but did not refund. "Trade Assurance is not a guaranteed refund" — [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/12-common-supplier-scams-how-to-avoids-fraud-when-sourcing)

**Made-in-China Secured Trading Service (buyer terms; version updated 2025-06-19, valid from 2025-06-26)**
- Lists "disputes over quality, payment and after-sales service" among disputes handled, but does not define coverage for quality, non-delivery or late shipment, and states no day-based dispute windows; disputes follow a separate "Standards for Settlement of Disputes" document not reproduced — [MIC Secured Trading Terms (Buyer)](https://m.made-in-china.com/trade/agreement.html)
- Refunds go back to the original payment route "within 5-10 days" — [MIC Terms](https://m.made-in-china.com/trade/agreement.html)
- A per-order transaction fee applies under a published schedule and is not returned if the seller refunds; MIC can change fee rules at its discretion — [MIC Terms](https://m.made-in-china.com/trade/agreement.html); buyer service itself is free but a fee applies on online payment, listed by payment method — [MIC Help Center](https://m.made-in-china.com/html/forApp/helpcenter/en.html)
- Buyer bears losses from suspected fraud, collusion, or failure to provide evidence; the terms do not address off-platform payment explicitly — [MIC Terms](https://m.made-in-china.com/trade/agreement.html)

### Inferences
- For a 5-unit order, the real protection is: pay by credit card through Alibaba checkout (2.99% fee, plus card chargeback rights as a second layer), and put specs, kit contents, color, packaging, ship-by date and "express door-to-door to Florida" in the on-platform order. A spec that is only in chat is weak evidence.
- The 30-day window starts at delivery, so the kit must be inspected and photographed on arrival (unboxing video) the same week.
- Made-in-China's protection is materially weaker on paper; prefer Alibaba TA for a first order unless the supplier is only on MIC.
- A US supplier has no such escrow; the protection there is a credit card (chargeback) and written terms.

### Gaps
- Official Alibaba TA dispute window, late-shipment compensation formula and fee schedule as of Nov 2026: in the TA T&Cs/guide PDF, not read. Third-party figures above are ESTIMATED.
- MIC transaction fee percentage: UNKNOWN (fee chart not retrieved).
- Whether Alibaba's TA refunds DDP duties or shipping if goods are rejected: UNKNOWN.

## 2. Verified / Gold Supplier / audited badges, and trading company vs factory

### Takeaway
Gold Supplier is a paid membership, not a quality signal. "Verified Supplier" means a third-party inspector (Alibaba names Bureau Veritas and TUV Rheinland) confirmed the company exists, its license and contact details, and its declared business type; it does not prove the company makes the product, and a verified trading company carries the same badge as a verified factory.

### Cited Findings
- Alibaba's verification page names Bureau Veritas and TUV Rheinland as inspectors performing on-site checks; an "Assessed Supplier" tier is available to Gold Suppliers in mainland China, Taiwan and Hong Kong — [Alibaba verification services](https://activities.alibaba.com/alibaba/buyer/ab/safety_security/products/verification_services.php)
- Gold Supplier is tied to annual membership fees — [Alibaba Seller Blog, 2026](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/dried-fruit/gold-supplier-10-years-partnership-guide-alibaba-b2b)
- Verification checks business license, contact details and business type (trading company, manufacturer or both); a verified trading company carries the same badge as a verified manufacturer — [VeriSupplier analysis](https://verisupplier-repo.pages.dev/alibaba-supplier-risk-check) (low-authority site; consistent with Alibaba's own description)
- "Being a 'gold supplier' on Alibaba does not mean anything" (as a scam safeguard) — [Epic Sourcing](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/how-to-avoid-alibaba-scams-red-flags-us-importers)
- Factories typically mean lower price, better technical communication, more control, higher MOQ; trading companies source from several factories; many suppliers are hybrid — [Alibaba Seller Blog, 2026](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/sports-entertainment/iso9001-manufacturer-trading-company-guide-alibaba-b2b)
- SGS or Bureau Veritas factory audit reports indicate a professional third party physically visited the site; buyers should also check the license on the national registry — [Alibaba verification services](https://activities.alibaba.com/alibaba/buyer/ab/safety_security/products/verification_services.php); [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/what-are-alibaba-verified-suppliers)

### Inferences
- How to tell factory vs trader in practice: business scope on the license (manufacturing vs "trading/import-export"), registered address matching the factory address in the audit report, a live video walk of the production line making this product (rigid platform molding or board cutting, foam, fabric sewing), and the audit report's factory floor area and staff count. A trader is not disqualifying for a 5-unit order (traders often have stock and accept tiny orders), but for private label at 50 to 100 units the factory matters for quality control and future exclusivity.
- Ask for the audit report itself (PDF with the inspector's name and date), not just the badge.

### Gaps
- Exactly which fields the current BV/TUV verification checks (e.g., whether it confirms production of the listed product category): not confirmed from the official page.
- Cost of the Alibaba "Assessed Supplier" or Gold membership to suppliers: not researched (not needed by buyer).

## 3. Red flags (price, payment pressure, bank changes, video, photos, samples, claims)

### Takeaway
The consistently cited top red flags are: any request to pay off-platform or to a personal account, a change of bank details (the classic email-compromise fraud), a beneficiary name that doesn't match the licensed company, payment-method switches or urgency, prices far below other sellers, refusal to sell a sample, and the "perfect sample, worse bulk" quality fade.

### Cited Findings
- A late change in bank details should be treated as a high-risk event until verified by phone or video with a known contact, never email alone; scammers compromise supplier email and swap bank details at payment time — [Epic Sourcing, 2026](https://www.epicsourcing.co/epic-guide/verify-chinese-supplier-payment-details-avoid-wire-fraud-south-africa-2026); [China Importal](https://www.chinaimportal.com/?p=4590)
- Check the beneficiary name matches the company on Alibaba and its business license; a mismatch or a personal account is a red flag; bank location should fit the registered address — [Epic Sourcing](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/how-to-avoid-alibaba-scams-red-flags-us-importers); [Cogoport](https://www.cogoport.com/en/blogs/how-to-avoid-china-supplier-scams)
- Pressure tactics: sudden account change near payment date, urgency before a holiday or shipment, switching payment mode (bank to wallet) — [Cogoport](https://www.cogoport.com/en/blogs/how-to-avoid-china-supplier-scams)
- Prices far below market are a warning sign — [Epic Sourcing](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/how-to-avoid-alibaba-scams-red-flags-us-importers)
- "Legitimate suppliers will sell you a sample"; refusing samples or insisting on full payment first is a red flag; never pay by Western Union; never send funds outside Alibaba on a first order with a new factory — [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/12-common-supplier-scams-how-to-avoids-fraud-when-sourcing)
- Quality fade / bait-and-switch: perfect sample, bulk doesn't match (example: genuine leather sample, PU leather bulk); order several samples to check consistency — [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/alibaba-scams-common-scams-when-fba-product-sourcing)
- A buyer reported a supplier raising the order price 50% after creation, citing "mistakes" in the price list and adding large shipping charges — [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/12-common-supplier-scams-how-to-avoids-fraud-when-sourcing)
- License red flags: blurry or edited license images, refusing to send the full license, details not matching the public registry — [Sino Shipping](https://www.sino-shipping.com/th/sourcing-procurement-china/compliance/how-to-verify-supplier-business-licenses/)

### Inferences
- Refusing a live video call or only having stock/catalog photos was not found as a sourced red flag in what was read; it is a common-sense check (and directly relevant here because CLAUDE.md requires real-sample photos), but treat it as our own rule, ASSUMED.
- "Too good" claims to watch for this product: "fits all cars", load ratings without a test report, "patented" claims (also a design-copy risk under CLAUDE.md section 7), and "US warehouse" without a US address and test-order tracking.

### Gaps
- No primary Reddit seller threads were retrieved; seller-experience claims come via vendor blogs.
- No source quantified how often each red flag precedes fraud.

## 4. Golden sample, pre-shipment photos/video, and video factory tours

### Takeaway
Standard practice is: approve a sample, keep it (and give a duplicate to the inspector) as the reference "golden sample" that bulk is checked against, and require photos/video or an inspection before paying the balance. Sources found did not give a formal golden-sample protocol beyond this.

### Cited Findings
- Request a sample before signing the purchase order — [Cogoport](https://www.cogoport.com/en/blogs/how-to-avoid-china-supplier-scams)
- Hire a China-based inspector for every first order with a new supplier and give a duplicate sample to the inspector — [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/12-common-supplier-scams-how-to-avoids-fraud-when-sourcing)
- Order several samples to check consistency, because quality fade hits bulk orders — [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/alibaba-scams-common-scams-when-fba-product-sourcing)
- Alibaba is described as safe for a first order if payment stays on-platform, specs are documented carefully and the supplier is verified independently before production — [Yakkyofy, 2026](https://yakkyofy.com/is-alibaba-legit/)

### Inferences
- For the 5-kit batch (which CLAUDE.md treats as the sample): keep one unopened kit as the golden sample, photograph and measure every part (platform dimensions, thickness, weight, strap/buckle hardware, foam, fabric, door guards), and write those numbers into the 50 to 100 unit order as the spec. That turns the sample into evidence for a TA dispute.
- Before paying the balance on the 50 to 100 unit order: require a dated photo and video set of the finished, packed goods (including one unit loaded with a weight and the private-label packaging), filmed live on a video call if possible. This is free and covers most of what a small order needs.

### Gaps
- No primary source found specifying a standard pre-shipment photo/video checklist or the typical cost of a supplier-run video factory tour (normally free; UNKNOWN as a sourced fact).

## 5. Third-party inspection (QIMA, V-Trust, AsiaInspection): price per man-day and when worth it

### Takeaway
No official 2026 QIMA rate was published; QIMA says pricing is a flat day rate quoted on request. Third-party estimates for China pre-shipment inspection cluster around $200 to $350 per man-day all-in, which is 40% to 70% of a $500 order, so it is not worth it for 5 units but is commonly recommended for a first production order with a new factory.

### Cited Findings
- QIMA: fees depend on service type, location and number of man-days; most inspections are a flat day rate with no hidden fees; quote within 24 hours — [QIMA product inspections](https://qima.com/consumer-products/product-inspections)
- Estimates (conflicting, ESTIMATED): $130 to $320 per man-day in China, realistic all-in $199 to $268 — [MetricRig, 2026](https://metricrig.pages.dev/answers/quality-control-inspection-cost-china-2026); $250 to $350 per man-day — [Supplyia](https://www.supplyia.com/inspection-company-in-china/); QIMA from $299 per man-day in China, all-in status not stated — [Sino Shipping](https://www.sino-shipping.com/id/jasa-kargo-dari-china/inspeksi/perusahaan-inspeksi-pihak-ketiga-sgs-bureau-veritas/); pre-shipment inspection USD 200 to 300 — [Epic Sourcing, 2026](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/24-04-how-to-use-alibaba-trade-assurance-a-buyers-safety-guide)
- Recommendation from sellers (via vendor blog): inspect every first order with a new supplier — [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/12-common-supplier-scams-how-to-avoids-fraud-when-sourcing)

### Inferences
- 5 units: skip third-party inspection; the kit opened at home is the inspection.
- 50 to 100 units (perhaps $1,000 to $3,000 of goods, ASSUMED): one man-day (about $200 to $350, ESTIMATED) is 10% to 30% of order value. Worth it if the factory is new and the order is paid mostly upfront; the cheaper substitute is detailed photo/video plus holding the balance (e.g., 30/70) until those are approved. Ask the inspector for a load test of the platform against the golden sample.
- Budget: CLAUDE.md's $250 samples line cannot also fund an inspection; it would come from supplier working capital or contingency (Dhruv's decision).

### Gaps
- Official QIMA, V-Trust and AsiaInspection (now part of QIMA brand history; not verified) 2026 prices: UNKNOWN; get a written quote.
- Whether inspectors travel-surcharge for factories outside main hubs: UNKNOWN.

## 6. Business license check (GSXT/NECIPS), supplier age, response rate and transaction metrics

### Takeaway
Ask for the business license, then look up its 18-character Unified Social Credit Code on gsxt.gov.cn (Chinese-only, CAPTCHA) and match legal name, legal representative, status, registered capital, address and business scope against the Alibaba profile, contract and bank beneficiary. Tianyancha and Qichacha aggregate the same data in a friendlier way.

### Cited Findings
- Search gsxt.gov.cn (NECIPS, run by SAMR) by the 18-character USCC or exact Chinese legal name; the USCC is more reliable than name search — [Hicom Asia](https://www.hicom-asia.com/how-to-check-chinese-company-necips/); [China Justice Observer](https://www.chinajusticeobserver.com/a/how-to-check-china-company-registration-number)
- The record shows legal name, registration status (active/deregistered), registered capital, legal representative — [Sino Shipping](https://www.sino-shipping.com/th/sourcing-procurement-china/compliance/how-to-verify-supplier-business-licenses/)
- The site is in Chinese and needs a CAPTCHA — [Hicom Asia](https://www.hicom-asia.com/how-to-check-chinese-company-necips/)
- Cross-check USCC, legal name and representative across license, registry record, contract, company chop and invoices; any mismatch is a reason to stop — [New Buying Agent](https://www.newbuyingagent.com/resources/how-to-read-a-chinese-business-license-and-verify-a-supplier)
- Tianyancha and Qichacha aggregate GSXT data; useful but not authoritative — [Sino Shipping](https://www.sino-shipping.com/th/sourcing-procurement-china/compliance/how-to-verify-supplier-business-licenses/)

### Inferences
- The registration date on GSXT gives true company age (the "X yrs on Alibaba" figure is only platform tenure). The business scope tells you whether it is licensed to manufacture.
- Alibaba profile metrics (response rate, response time, on-time delivery rate, number of transactions, store rating) are useful screening filters for a 5-unit order, but no source found defines how Alibaba calculates them; treat them as supplier-platform claims, not verified facts.
- For a US supplier, the equivalent checks are the state Secretary of State business search, a real warehouse address (not a residential or virtual address), and a test order with tracking.

### Gaps
- No official Alibaba definition of "on-time delivery rate" or "response rate" was retrieved: UNKNOWN.
- No source gave a threshold (e.g., minimum years or transaction count) that experienced sellers use; any threshold would be ASSUMED.

## 7. Shipping: express door-to-door, duties, DDP vs DAP, tariffs on this product, end of de minimis

### Takeaway
De minimis is gone: for China origin since 2025-05-02 and for all countries since 2025-08-29, so even a 5-unit express parcel to Florida is a dutiable entry with brokerage charges. After the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs (2026-02-20), Chinese goods now carry MFN duty plus any original Section 301 list duty plus a 12.5% Section 301 "forced-labor" tariff (since 2026-07-24), unless Section 232 applies instead. The big unknown is this product's HTS code: CBP classified a fabric rear-seat protector under 8708.99.8180 (auto accessory, 2.5% + 25% Section 301), not 6307.90, and 8708 codes may also touch Section 232 programs. Get the code confirmed before quoting landed cost.

### Cited Findings
**De minimis**
- China/Hong Kong de minimis ended 2025-05-02 — [UPS tariffs page](https://www.ups.com/cn/en/shipping/international-shipping/tariffs)
- Executive Order 14324 suspended duty-free de minimis for all countries effective 12:01 a.m. 2025-08-29; non-postal shipments need a formal or informal entry in ACE with all duties, taxes and fees paid; CBP may require a basic importation bond for informal entries of $2,500 or less — [CBP CSMS bulletin](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/3f01456); [Thompson Hine, July 2025](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2025/07/president-suspends-duty-free-de-minimis-imports-for-all-countries-and-imposes-new-tariffs-on-low-value-shipments/)
- One 2026 timeline says the suspension was explicitly continued in February 2026 — [Branvas](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/de-minimis-end-small-ecommerce-impact) (secondary; not verified against CBP)

**Tariffs on Chinese goods (as of the latest sources, July 2026)**
- Supreme Court ruled 2026-02-20 that IEEPA does not authorize tariffs; IEEPA fentanyl (cut to 10% on 2025-11-10) and reciprocal tariffs no longer collected; a 10% Section 122 surcharge ran 2026-02-24 to 2026-07-24; a 12.5% Section 301 forced-labor tariff on Chinese goods took effect 2026-07-24 and does not apply to goods already under Section 232 — [China Briefing, updated 2026-07-24](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/us-china-tariff-rates-2025/)
- Original Section 301 lists still apply: 25% on Lists 1 to 3, 7.5% on List 4A — [China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/us-china-tariff-rates-2025/)
- Same page notes an internal inconsistency on whether Section 122 was 10% or 15%, and that a Section 301 excess-capacity investigation (launched March 2026) was ongoing with no action announced — [China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/us-china-tariff-rates-2025/)
- Some sources describe China's forced-labor tier differently (10% vs 12.5% tiers) but agree China is at 12.5%; 178 Section 301 exclusions were set to expire 2026-11-09 unless extended — [Argents](https://argents.com/section-301-tariffs-2026-guide/); [EximAgent](https://eximagent.ai/blog/us-china-trade-policy-2026-or-stalled-tariffs-force-new-strategy)

**Classification of this product type**
- CBP ruling N325857 (2022-05-17): a rear-seat "Seat Protector" (polyester with foam, drapes over rear bench, configurable as a hammock) classified 8708.99.8180, general duty 2.5%, plus 25% Section 301 under 9903.88.03; CBP rejected the importer's proposed 6307.90.9891 — [Ruling N325857](https://open-gov.usebase.io/rulings/N325857)
- Other rulings: car seat organizer and textile dog crate cover in 6307.90.9891 (2023); a 2014 car seat protector ruling referenced 6307.90.9889 and 8708.99.8180 — [CBP rulings search results via usebase](https://open-gov.usebase.io/rulings/N335048); [N015530](https://www.tarifflens.ai/rulings/N015530)
- Textile pet carriers are not classified in heading 4202 (Court of International Trade) — [CBP ruling H313073](https://open-gov.usebase.io/rulings/H313073)
- 8708.99.81 appears in a Federal Register Section 232 steel/aluminum derivative inclusions annex — [Federal Register 2025-15819](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2025-15819.pdf); the Section 232 auto parts annex includes chapter 87 codes, effective 2025-05-03, but no source read confirmed 8708.99.81 specifically on the auto parts list — [Thompson Hine](https://www.thompsonhine.com/insights/annex-identifying-scope-of-automobile-and-automobile-parts-subject-to-section-232-tariffs-is-released)

**Express shipping, who pays, DDP vs DAP**
- DHL raised clearing fees and tariff deposits for US-bound small parcels (Nomura report); DHL says CBP refunds where DHL was importer of record go to the party that paid the duties (relevant to the IEEPA refunds) — [DHL customs regulatory updates](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-au/logistics-advice/import-export-advice/customs-regulatory-updates)
- Industry guides call DDP the safer structure for ecommerce parcels from China; one blog example cites about $15 brokerage per FedEx parcel (unverified); formal entry budgeted at $150 to $350 — [Topway Shipping](https://www.topwayshipping.com/ship-from-china-to-the-us-in-2026-why-de-minimis-no-longer-saves-you-a-cent/); [Fulfillmen](https://fulfillmen.com/import-tax-from-china-to-usa); [Branvas](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/de-minimis-end-small-ecommerce-impact)
- No official 2026 DHL/FedEx/UPS China-to-US brokerage schedule found.

### Inferences
- DAP (delivered at place, duties unpaid): the courier bills Dhruv for duties, plus a brokerage/disbursement fee, before or after delivery; DHL/FedEx bill the receiver by default on express imports. DDP: supplier or its forwarder pays duties and quotes one all-in price. For a 5-unit order, ask the supplier for an express DDP quote and an itemized DAP alternative, and compare.
- Rough stack if 8708.99.81 applies: 2.5% MFN + 25% Section 301 (9903.88.03) + 12.5% forced-labor 301 = about 40% of customs value, before brokerage. If 6307.90.98 applies, the MFN is higher (about 7%, not verified this session) and its Section 301 list status was not confirmed. If any Section 232 program applies, the 12.5% does not stack but the 232 rate does. All of this is ESTIMATED and must be replaced with a broker's answer.
- A rigid extender (hard platform, possibly plastic, metal or wood board, plus fabric cover) may classify differently from a soft hammock: possibly 8708.99.81, or by the material giving essential character. Unknown until a customs broker reviews the spec.
- Watch for suppliers under-declaring value on the invoice to cut duties: it is the importer's legal exposure (Dhruv's), not theirs.

- HTS code for a rigid dog back-seat extender kit: UNKNOWN. Needs a customs broker opinion or a CBP binding ruling (CROSS search for "dog seat extender" / "back seat bridge" not run).
- Whether 8708.99.81 is inside the Section 232 auto parts tariff (25%) for this product: UNKNOWN.

- Tariff status after 2026-07-24 (e.g., excess-capacity action, any US-China deal at or around 2026-11-10, exclusion expiry on 2026-11-09): not confirmed as of 2026-11-18; latest source found is dated 2026-07-24.

## 8. What experienced sellers say about scams and failures with small first orders

### Takeaway
Small first orders fail more often through quality fade, price changes after ordering, surprise shipping charges and slow or partial refunds than through outright theft; outright fraud mostly comes through off-platform payment and bank-detail changes. The common advice is test small ($500 to $1,000), stay on-platform, and document specs.

### Cited Findings
- Test with $500 to $1,000 rather than committing $10,000 on a first order — [Yakkyofy, 2026](https://yakkyofy.com/is-alibaba-legit/)
- Quality fade: approved sample, mismatched bulk — [CosmoSourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/alibaba-scams-common-scams-when-fba-product-sourcing)

### Inferences
Practical checklist by order size (costs are ESTIMATED or ASSUMED as labeled):

**Before any money (free, about 1 to 2 hours per supplier)**
1. License photo, then GSXT/Qichacha lookup by USCC: name, status, registration date, scope, legal rep, address.
2. Match beneficiary name on the Alibaba order to the licensed company. Refuse any off-platform, personal, or "new" account; verify any bank change by video call.
3. Live video call: show this exact product, the platform being made or stock on a shelf, and the packing area. Ask for unedited photos of a real unit (not catalog images).
4. Ask for verification/audit report PDF; note factory vs trader.
5. Compare price against 3 or more other quotes; reject outliers far below.
6. Written specs in the on-platform order: dimensions, materials, load rating, contents of kit, color, plain packaging/blind shipping, ship-by date, express DDP to Florida with tracking.

**5-unit mini batch (under $500)**
7. Pay by credit card through Alibaba TA (about 2.99% fee, ESTIMATED). Do not wire.
8. Get express DDP quote vs DAP; confirm who pays duties and the HTS code on the commercial invoice; confirm declared value is true.
9. On arrival: unboxing video, measure, photograph, load test; open any TA dispute within 30 days of delivery (ESTIMATED window).
10. Keep one unopened kit as golden sample. No third-party inspection (cost about $200 to $350, ESTIMATED, not justified).

**50 to 100 unit private-label order**
11. Specs and golden-sample photos referenced in the order; private-label artwork approved in writing; packaging proof photo before mass production.
12. Payment split (commonly 30% deposit / 70% before shipment, ASSUMED, not sourced this session) all through TA.
13. Pre-shipment: dated photo/video set of finished goods; consider one man-day inspection (about $200 to $350, ESTIMATED) if factory is new and value is above roughly $1,500 (ASSUMED threshold).
14. Customs broker confirms HTS and duty rate before ordering; get freight quote (express vs air freight) with duties itemized.
15. Identify a backup supplier (CLAUDE.md section 2).
