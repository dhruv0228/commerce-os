## Channel-by-channel comparison for this case

### Takeaway
No single channel delivers all of: 5 units, own brand, US stock, and under 10 days. Platform-direct messaging and RFQs (Alibaba and Made-in-China) are free and reach factories with MOQs of 2 to 5 at about $12 to $22 a unit. They ship from China, though, so speed depends on paid express. US-stock options (CJ US warehouse, AliExpress US-ship, US resellers like IBSPOT) are fast but unbranded. Agents and customs-record lookups matter at the bulk and private-label stage, not at 5 units.

### Cited Findings

**1. Alibaba: messaging individual suppliers vs RFQ ("Request for Quotation"); Trade Assurance**
- How RFQ works: the buyer writes one spec (product, quantity, quality, delivery, value-added services such as branding), posts it in My Alibaba > Buying Request Hub, Alibaba screens it, and suppliers who choose to reply send quotes. The article lists four RFQ types: invited, open, sealed bid and reverse auction. — [Wise, "How to create and submit an Alibaba RFQ" (dated 14 May 2022; OLDER, flag)](https://wise.com/us/blog/alibaba-rfq)
- Cost and limits: free users get 10 RFQ quotations; a paid RFQ subscription gives more (price not stated). Suppliers may not reply at all; Wise advises sending to 5 to 10 vendors, confirming the price is final and not a reference price, and checking incoterms (EXW vs DAP). — [Wise (2022)](https://wise.com/us/blog/alibaba-rfq)
- Response time: one guide claims RFQ quotes arrive "within 24 hours at the most", and a seller-side benchmark is to answer RFQs within 24 hours. These are anecdotes and seller targets, not guarantees. — [Shopify blog, Alibaba 101](https://www.shopify.com/blog/16665772-alibaba-101-how-to-safely-source-products-from-the-worlds-biggest-supplier-directory); [Alibaba seller blog, How to use RFQ (June 2021; OLDER)](https://seller.alibaba.com/businessblogs/how-to-use-rfq-a-detailed-guide-with-examples-px97m41v)
- Trade Assurance: escrow-style buyer protection, described as free to buyers. It applies only when payment is made on Alibaba.com (wire transfer is the stated exception), and coverage is capped by a per-supplier Trade Assurance limit. Alibaba markets "100% payment, product quality and on-time shipment protection", but a third-party guide notes refunds are not automatic on the buyer's request. — [Guided Imports, Trade Assurance overview](https://guidedimports.com/blog/alibaba-trade-assurance-overview/); [Epic Sourcing, Trade Assurance guide](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/how-to-use-alibaba-trade-assurance-your-complete-safety-guide-for-global-bu); [Alibaba seller blog, Trade Assurance complete guide (2026)](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/b2b-trade/trade-assurance-complete-guide-alibaba-secure-payment)
- Low-MOQ extender suppliers already found on Alibaba: Huizhou Ouqi, $17.88 to $26.87, MOQ 2 (card read); Guangzhou Doglemi, $21.67, MOQ 5 (snippet). No US stock found on Alibaba. — repo: `research/sourcing-2026-11-17/README.md`

**4. Sourcing agents (China)**
- Jingsourcing (official pricing page, no date shown, © 2026): Basic plan is a 5% commission with a $100 minimum fee. Pro plan is 0% commission, with the margin "included in the unit price". Each product must be at least $500 of purchase value (averaged across the order); below that there is a $25 handling fee per product. Standard AQL 2.5 inspection is free. Photos start at $3 each. — [Jingsourcing pricing](https://jingsourcing.com/pricing/)
- Leeline Sourcing (pricing post dated 3 Jan 2023; OLDER, flag): orders under $1,000 pay a $100 flat fee, 100% upfront. Rates are 10% for $1,001 to $3,000, 9% for $3,001 to $6,000, down to 6% above $20,000. Dropship processing is $1 to $2 per order. Inspections start at $300. 1688 buying is listed as a service, with no fee shown. Storage terms conflict (1 month free vs 3 months waived). — [Leeline pricing](https://www.leelinesourcing.com/pricing/)
- Supplyia (own page): commission is typically 5% to 10% of order value, falling as order size rises. No minimum is stated. — [Supplyia China sourcing agent](https://www.supplyia.com/China-sourcing-agent/)
- Industry ranges (agent-run blogs): 3% to 15% commission, with most full-service agents at 5% to 10%. Minimum service fees are often about $150 to $450 per order. Many professional agents will not take orders under $5,000 on commission. Watch for undisclosed factory kickbacks when an agent quotes below 3%. Common hidden fees are inspection days, sample express freight and destination clearance. — [Maple Sourcing, fees breakdown](https://maplesourcing.com/china-sourcing-agent-fees-complete-cost-breakdown.html); [Epic Sourcing, US agent fees 2026](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/sourcing-agent-fees-usa-2026); [Cosmo Sourcing](https://www.cosmosourcing.com/blog/what-is-a-sourcing-agent)
- 1688.com: claimed to be 10% to 40% cheaper than Alibaba, but it is Chinese-language only and has no Trade Assurance. An agent is the usual way in. — [SellerSprite, Alibaba supplier guide 2026](https://cn.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-fba-alibaba-supplier-guide-2026)
- NicheDropshipping: pricing page returned 404, so terms are UNKNOWN.
- Upwork and Fiverr sourcing agents: no sourced data gathered. See Gaps.

**6. Customs-record lookup (ImportYeti, Panjiva, ImportGenius)**
- Method: (1) find the competitor's legal importer name (if the brand name returns nothing, use a trademark search such as Justia to find the registrant); (2) search it on ImportYeti; (3) review the supplier list, shipment frequency and volume; (4) judge fit, because big-brand factories may not suit a small first order. — [MyWifeQuitHerJob, ImportYeti guide (undated; images 2019 and 2022)](https://mywifequitherjob.com/importyeti/)
- Cost: ImportYeti is free (donations). Panjiva has charged $299/month in the past. ImportGenius is paid. Jungle Scout bundles a supplier database. — [MyWifeQuitHerJob](https://mywifequitherjob.com/importyeti/)
- Limits: US sea imports only, and data starts in 2015. Importers can hide via an exporter-of-record service or CBP confidential treatment (renewed every 2 years). Contact details are often missing, so operators search the factory name on Alibaba, Made-in-China or the factory's site and reach out there. — [MyWifeQuitHerJob](https://mywifequitherjob.com/importyeti/); [Bellingcat toolkit, ImportYeti](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit/more/all-tools/importyeti)
- Already applied in the repo: Ningbo Inbest Homeware (4Knines) and Zhejiang Treasureland Auto (Ruff Liners) were found via public customs aggregators, and outreach is drafted "for the bigger order". — repo: `suppliers/SEND_LIST.md`

- The 5-unit "agent" cost is the minimum fee, not the percentage. Jingsourcing works out to max(5% × order, $100) + $25 per product under $500 (Basic plan). Leeline is a $100 flat fee under $1,000. ## Which channel do experienced Amazon private-label sellers and DTC builders recommend in 2025-2026 for a fast small first order?

### Takeaway
The consistent advice (from seller blogs and agent sites, not Reddit, which could not be reached via search) is to go direct on Alibaba first, pay for samples and a small trial run, check whether the supplier is a factory or a trading company, and bring in an agent only once language, QC or off-platform factories become the bottleneck. For speed to a US address, US-stock marketplace buys are the only sub-10-day route, but they don't give you your own brand.

### Cited Findings
- "Alibaba is where most Amazon sellers find manufacturers". Search with the product name plus "manufacturer" and check badges to tell factories from trading companies. — [SellerSprite, Alibaba supplier guide 2026](https://cn.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-fba-alibaba-supplier-guide-2026)
- For a small batch or a test in a new category, a sourcing agent "may not be cost-effective". Hire one only after you have tried to find factories yourself. — [Supplyia, what is a sourcing agency](https://www.supplyia.com/what-is-a-sourcing-agency/) (agent-run site); [Epic Sourcing, FBA sourcing guide](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/15-05-amazon-fba-product-sourcing-china-complete-guide)
- Smaller factories offer "lower MOQs, free customization, faster reorders". This is the view of a course-selling seller, so treat it as opinion. — [MyWifeQuitHerJob, Amazon sourcing secrets](https://mywifequitherjob.com/amazon-fba-sourcing-secrets/)
- Use the sample as the quality gate: "if a sample has defects, the mass order is likely to be worse". Ask for a video walkthrough of the production line. Start with 100 to 200 units for the first real order. — [Epic Sourcing FBA guide](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/15-05-amazon-fba-product-sourcing-china-complete-guide); [Helium 10, sourcing for FBA](https://www.helium10.com/project-x/sourcing-product-for-amazon-fba/)
- Customs-record lookup (ImportYeti) is the widely taught way to find the factory behind a competitor, and it is free. — [MyWifeQuitHerJob, ImportYeti](https://mywifequitherjob.com/importyeti/)

### Inferences
- The project's current plan (direct messages to MOQ-2 to MOQ-5 factories, plus customs-record factories for bulk) matches the mainstream operator advice. The one addition worth considering is a single Alibaba RFQ post, which is free and casts a wider net.
- The 5-kit express order is unusual against operator norms (100 to 200 units). Expect some factories to decline or to quote samples rather than an order. Treat the 5 units as "samples at sample prices".

## What do sourcing agents charge, and is it worth it at 5 units?

### Takeaway
No. Published agent pricing has a floor of about $100 per order (Jingsourcing Basic: 5% with a $100 minimum, plus $25 per product under $500; Leeline: $100 flat under $1,000), and the industry quotes $150 to $450 minimum service fees. On a 5-kit order worth roughly $60 to $110 in goods, the fee would be about equal to or more than the product cost.

### Cited Findings
- Jingsourcing: Basic is 5% with a $100 minimum. Pro is 0% commission, with the margin built into the unit price. The minimum is $500 per product, or a $25 handling fee per product. — [Jingsourcing pricing (© 2026)](https://jingsourcing.com/pricing/)
- Leeline: $100 flat under $1,000, 100% upfront; 10% for $1,001 to $3,000; 6% above $20,000; inspections from $300. — [Leeline pricing (Jan 2023, OLDER)](https://www.leelinesourcing.com/pricing/)
- Supplyia: 5% to 10%, decreasing with order size. — [Supplyia](https://www.supplyia.com/China-sourcing-agent/)
- Industry: 3% to 15% (mostly 5% to 10%); minimum service fees about $150 to $450; agents often decline commission work under $5,000; beware kickbacks below 3%. — [Maple Sourcing](https://maplesourcing.com/china-sourcing-agent-fees-complete-cost-breakdown.html); [Epic Sourcing US fees 2026](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/sourcing-agent-fees-usa-2026)

### Inferences
- The overhead math (5 kits × $12.40 to $21.67 from the repo's listings = about $62 to $108 in goods vs a $100+ agent fee) is illustrative arithmetic on ESTIMATED prices. Any real comparison should run in `scripts/economics.py`.
- Agents become worth it at the bulk or private-label order (likely $1,000+): factory audit, AQL inspection (free at Jingsourcing), consolidation, and 1688 access for prices 10% to 40% lower. "Pro" models with 0% commission hide the margin in the unit price, so compare them against a direct factory quote.

### Gaps
- NicheDropshipping and Upwork or Fiverr freelance agent rates: not found (404 / not researched).
