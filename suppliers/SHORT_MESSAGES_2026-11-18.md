# Short supplier messages (v2, 2026-11-18)

Replaces the long drafts in each `suppliers/*/inquiry.md` for first contact. Based on `reports/How sellers contact suppliers.md`. The long drafts' extra questions (packaging, defect terms, bed topper, door-guard pricing, HS code) move to round 2. Dhruv sends; Claude never sends. The copy-ready version with ZIP and email lives outside the repo.

```

Hello,

I'm Dhruv, founder of Barkfare, a US dog travel brand. I'd like to sell your hard-bottom dog car seat cover with the 7-layer bottom plate under our brand.

Please reply with:
1. Unit price at 5, 50 and 100 units

3. Express shipping cost and days to Florida, duties paid
4. Our logo on product and box: minimum and cost
5. Production time for 100 units
6. Factory or trading company?

Real photos of it folded would help. We'll decide within 2 weeks.

Thanks,
Dhruv Patel
Founder, Barkfare
```
