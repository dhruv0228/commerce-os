## Claims we can make (only once the sample proves them)

- "Hard bottom that doesn't sag." Needs a filmed kneel test on our sample.
- "Fits most cars, SUVs and trucks." Needs real measurements and the fit finder.
- "Door guards that never go in your window seal." True by design.
- "Waterproof." Only if the supplier's fabric spec and our water test confirm it.
- "Washable." Only if the cover comes off and the supplier confirms the care label.
- A weight rating, only as measured on our sample. Never copy the 280 lb or 400 lb marketing numbers from listings.

## Claims we never make

- Safety or crash-protection claims ("keeps your dog safe in a crash"). The tether is a restraint aid, not crash-tested.
- Anxiety or health claims ("cures car anxiety", "vet recommended") unless a real vet says it, with permission.
- "Patented", "#1", "best seller" or "as seen on" unless it is true for us.
- A "free" item with a dollar value until we have actually sold that item separately at that price (FTC guide on "free" claims). Until then, write "door guards included".
- Strike-through "was" prices we never charged (FTC guides against deceptive pricing).
- Reviews we didn't earn: no imported reviews, no staff or family reviews without disclosure, no AI people (FTC 16 CFR Part 465 and 255).
