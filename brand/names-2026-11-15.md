## Known conflicts

- WAGABONZ trademark record (trademarks.justia.com, serial 90351293): different spelling, goods UNKNOWN.
- PAWSENGER, serial 98385431, Class 35: reported dead (markinton.com).
- PAWSEAT (97390808) and PAWSINCAR (98881503): pet car-seat marks. Avoid names close to these.
- Houndworthy: an existing outdoor dog brand (mrssizzle.com, 2014), so it was dropped.
- Roadhound and Ride Along were dropped earlier (existing marks in the class).
