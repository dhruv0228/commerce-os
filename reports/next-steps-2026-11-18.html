Question 4

One product or several? One hero now

Launch the extender kit alone, with the bed topper and door guards as add-ons. Spreading $1,800 across several new products means none gets enough ad money to prove anything. The other products come after the first 10 sales, tested by dropshipping first so you don't hold inventory.
Question 5

What competitors' ads look like

Live and recent ads for back-seat extenders (Foreplay, Nov 14 and 15). “Days running” means the brand kept paying for it, which is a good sign but not proof of profit.

What wins: a question about the problem over a real dog in a real car (“Does your dog slide around?”, “dirty paws?”), “not all extenders are equal” comparisons, and bundles with a free bed.
