## The model: hybrid
- **Lead with TikTok Shop.** Creator affiliates earn about 13.5% commission (pets standard rate, ESTIMATED) only when they sell. Dhruv's labeled AI-twin demo videos run from the shop account.
- **Run the Amboras store alongside.** It carries bundles, accessories and refills, captures email, and gets Meta only for retargeting.
- **Add Amazon FBA for proven winners.**
- **Why:** acquisition becomes a share of each sale, instead of paying Meta $38 to $52 per buyer up front.

- **Test batch:** shipped by express air, landed cost is about $83 per kit, so every test channel loses money. Get DDP or consolidated-air quotes before buying.
- **For $10k a month:** about 240 to 290 scaled orders a month.

## The loop
1. Buy a batch.
2. Seed creators and post AI-twin demos.
3. Sell on TikTok Shop and the store.
4. Get paid (new TikTok sellers wait about 31 days).
5. Reinvest in a bigger batch by sea and more creators.

## Examples to copy
- **Ruff Liners** (same product, hybrid): about $1.25M in 2023 (REPORTED).
- **MasterPieces** (TikTok creator process): up to $35K a month (CLAIMED). Read it with the Swoveralls warning: more than $2 spent per $1 of sales.
- **BrüMate** (tested demand with $20 to $30 a day of Meta ads before stock): about 30% profit after ads (REPORTED).
- **WeLoveDoodles** (first product picked from sales data): about 500 units for $2,500 (REPORTED).
- **RuffRover** (general store that pivoted to one hero): 3,735 reviews (VERIFIED).
