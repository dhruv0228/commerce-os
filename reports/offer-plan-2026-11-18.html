Reading the numbers
  • The first air-freight batch barely makes money on any tier. That's expected: it's a test. The tiers matter most once we ship by sea.
  • TikTok at test stage includes free creator samples, so it looks worse there. At scale, TikTok and our own store come out about the same.

The product bible

Saved as brand/product-bible.md. Every ad, page and AI prompt reads it first. What it holds:
  • Who it's for, the one-line promise, the brand voice.
  • The kit spec, each item marked as assumed until the sample proves it.
  • Claims we can make only after filming proof (no sag, fit, waterproof), and claims we never make (crash safety, health, fake “was” prices, “free $40” before we've sold it).
  • Guarantee, returns and warranty drafts. The patents to stay clear of.
  • Faces: creators with the real sample; your AI twin as voice only, never claiming use.
