What Davie Fogarty's video gets right, and what we take from it

“If I Wanted to Hit $1M/Month in Ecommerce, I'd Do This,” Davie Fogarty Mentors, 8:44, premiered Nov 15, 2026. Based on the full transcript and your 4 screenshots.

The verdict

Where we're aheadHis video starts after you already have a product, a supplier and sales. We did those parts properly:
  • Product chosen from real sales data
  • Costs and break-even worked out before buying
  • Suppliers found and ranked
Where he's aheadThese are the levers that turn visitors into buyers. We haven't built them yet:
  • A heavy offer stack
  • A product page copied from the winners and tested
  • A weekly ad-volume target
  • A daily profit tracker
The real hack

He doesn't ask Claude for one answer. He asks for 3 options, then corrects them with what he can actually deliver, then asks to see it as a page before deciding. That loop works for anything.

His 3 steps, decoded

STEP 1Fix the offer and product page
  • Copy what the top direct-to-consumer brands do (he uses Gruns)
  • Bundles, a pre-selected best deal, price per day or per serving, free shipping, a guarantee
  • Have Claude Code rebuild the page, 3 versions, then split-test
STEP 2Launch more ads, every week
  • Ads wear out in about 2 months now (his coaches' estimate)
  • Set a weekly target for new ads (20, then 30, then 40)
  • Name ads the same way every time, and track win rate
STEP 3Track profit every day
  • True landed cost per product, updated monthly
  • Two targets: MER (sales divided by ad spend) and the minimum MER to make your profit target
  • Look at it daily before touching ad budgets

His profit target, with our numbers

MER = total sales divided by total ad spend. Below the break-even MER, ads lose money. The target adds our 25% safety cushion. Computed from our fee model for the own store with Meta ads at $149.

Test batch (air freight)5.08target MER · break-even 3.81
At scale (sea freight)2.79target MER · break-even 2.09

In plain words: during the test, every $1 of ads needs to bring in about $5 of sales. Once we buy in bulk by sea, about $2.80 is enough. That's why the big order matters.

Things to be careful with
  • It's a sales funnel too: the video ends in his paid mentorship. “Profitable from day one” is one client story, not proof.
  • Prize draws are risky: his “monthly draw” is a sweepstakes. Those have state rules, and large prizes can require registration in some states, Florida included. We skip it.
  • Strike-through prices must be real: a “was $149.85” price has to be one people actually paid. Ours will be (FTC rules on price comparisons and “free” offers).
  • Photos off the internet: fine for a private mockup only. Live pages use photos of our real sample.
  • 20 to 40 ads a week assumes a big budget. Our version: 3 to 5 new creator videos a week, tracked the same way.
