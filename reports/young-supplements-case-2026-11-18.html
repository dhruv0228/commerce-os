Why it worked

1A face people already trustHudson is famous in exactly the world his customers live in. That's free reach and instant credibility. Most of the brand's power comes from here.
2One sharp promiseNot “a better pre-workout” but “it won't get you banned.” A real fear, a true story, and proof (lab tests, a government survey showing 1 in 3 supplements failed).
3People buy it againSupplements run out, so customers come back every month. Subscribe & save locks that in. Each customer is worth far more than one order.
4The offer does the sellingPer-serve pricing, the big pack pre-selected, free shipping and free guides that cost them almost nothing. Davie's playbook, live.
5Two buyers, one productThe parents' guide speaks to the person paying, not just the athlete. Smart, and easy to copy.

How we do something similar

We can't sell supplements (our rules rule out anything you eat or drink). We can copy the shape of the business:

Step 1 · heroBack-seat extender kitThe product with proven sales. Offer stack, guarantee, founder-style story.
Step 2 · add-onsSame dog ownerBed topper, door guards, seat-belt tether, travel bowl. Tested by dropshipping first, no inventory.
Step 3 · reordersSomething that runs outPaw wipes or a car odor remover: the dog-car version of the supplement tub. The scanner showed odor eliminators sell about 539,000 a month on Amazon at around $15.
Step 4 · bundlesBigger orders“Road trip set” (kit + bed + wipes), “two-car pack.”
Step 5 · next heroDog car ramp or stairsSells at $60 to $110, perfect for the same owner once their dog gets older.
The missing piece: our “Hudson.” His fame does a lot of the work. We don't have that, and you don't want to be on camera. Options:
  • Partner with a real, credible dog person (trainer, groomer, a dog creator with a following) as the brand face, paid with commission or a small share.
  • Make the dog the star: a brand dog with its own page and running story.
  • Your AI twin can speak as the founder, but it can never claim to have used the product. The real story has to come from real people.
Honest caveats
  • “Profitable from day one on Facebook” is Davie's claim about his own client. I found no numbers to check it.
  • Hudson's audience and story can't be copied. The offer, the page and the product ladder can.
  • Prices are in Australian dollars.
