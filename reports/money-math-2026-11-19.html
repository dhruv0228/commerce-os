Barkfare money math

A $149 kit makes about $34.25 profit per order once we buy in bulk. The 5-kit trial loses a little money on purpose: it buys proof that people will pay $149 and that the supplier delivers.

All numbers come from scripts/money_math.py. Costs are estimates until suppliers quote. Ads use the pet-category average of $38.51 per sale from established stores.

Is this dropshipping?

Only at the very start, and only as a test. Classic dropshipping means a supplier in China mails each order from China after the customer pays, and the parcel takes 2 to 4 weeks. We don't do that. Here is how an order moves at each stage:

The big catch is cash: stock and ads are paid before customer money arrives, so we grow in steps, funded by profit.

What could change these numbers

  • Product cost is an estimate. Supplier quotes replace it. For 5 kits it may be higher per kit than shown.
  • Import duty could be 27% or 52.5%, depending on how customs classifies the kit. The third row of the chart shows the high case.
  • Shipping from home ($20.55 by USPS) is an estimate for a 9 lb box. The real box size from the supplier changes it.
  • Refunds are assumed at 6%.
  • Ad cost is the category average for established stores. A new store usually pays more at first.
  • The fast lane looks cheapest because the seller's price includes shipping. It's an unverified seller with 9 sales, and the product isn't ours, so it's a test, not the business.
