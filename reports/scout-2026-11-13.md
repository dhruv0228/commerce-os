## 1. Executive summary

- **108 ideas considered:** 38 became candidates, 70 were rejected in discovery. The research covered 4 category groups (desk/organization, home/kitchen/cleaning, pet/auto/travel, outdoor/fitness/hobby).
- **The ad-cost bar is high.** Meta median cost per purchase is $38.99 overall and $47.93 for Home & Garden (Triple Whale, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026). To pass the 25% cushion, a kit needs roughly $55 to $65 of profit before ads, which means kit prices around $119 to $149.
- **No candidate reaches the 75-point bar** on current evidence. Top scores:
  - Pet portrait bundle: 72
  - Desk Reset Kit: 66
  - Filtered Shower Kit: 66 (uncorrected estimates)
  - Espresso puck-prep kit: 64 after deep-dive corrections
  - All of these are preliminary. Real supplier quotes will move them by several points either way.
- **The most important finding is about the business model.** For physical kits we found **no US-warehouse dropship supplier** that stocks the parts and ships them as one box. The realistic paths are:
  1. **US print-on-demand:** no inventory, made to order in the US. This fits the current rules.
  2. **A small inventory buy** (20 to 50 kits packed at the factory) sent to a no-minimum US 3PL or Amazon MCF. This needs an exception to the "no bulk inventory" rule.
- **Proposed house policy: no sitewide discounts.** With the 10% average discount assumed in the original brief, almost nothing passes. Without it, the strong candidates do. The policy is applied in every base scenario and needs your approval at Gate 1.
- **Recommendation (Gate 1, option A):** investigate two products in parallel at near-zero cost, through quotes and one sample each, then choose at Gate 5 (around Dec 20) on real numbers:
  - **Desk Reset Kit:** the stronger long-term brand. It fits the January timing, your own customer and your software edge, and it needs a small inventory buy.
  - **Pet portrait bundle:** the only path that stays inside today's no-inventory rule, but January is its weakest month.
  - **Parked:** espresso kit (thin margin, patent risk, needs inventory) and shower filter (health-claim exposure, crowded).
- **Confidence: low to medium.**

## 4. Deep analysis of the top 3

### Pet portrait bundle ($149: illustrated portrait on a 50x60 sherpa + 8x10 canvas + digital file)

- **Why it deserves investigation:**
  - US print-on-demand (Printful, Printify, Gooten) means no inventory and no tariffs on the print step, and it is already inside the hard rules.
  - Demand: "pet portrait" 12,100/mo and "pet memorial gifts" 8,100/mo on Google US.
  - **Your software edge is real here:** an instant AI-assisted preview before payment. Crown & Paw proofs take 1 to 2 days, West & Willow sends no proof, and Cuddle Clones takes about 4 weeks. Add a photo-quality checker and an automated POD order pipeline.
- **Economics (`economics_outputs.csv`):**
  - Base: $57.69 per order before ads, target cost per buyer $43.27 vs the $38.51 pet benchmark. Passes at 1.12.
  - High case: passes at 1.64.
  - Low case (10% discount, 10% refunds, higher art and print cost): fails at 0.23.
- **What would kill it:**
  1. **Price:** $149 is about 1.5x Crown & Paw's blanket plus canvas (about $100 before its standing 20 to 25% codes). The art has to look clearly better on the sample.
  2. **January is the trough.** Generic gift searches fall 70 to 90% after December, and only the memorial angle holds flat (8,100 in Jan). Mother's Day (May) is the next real window.
  3. **Delivery time:** two parcels (blanket and canvas ship separately) and 5 to 9 business days after art approval. That breaks the 2 to 8 day promise unless the page states it honestly.
  4. **AI-art backlash and copyright:** AI-only art likely isn't copyrightable by us. Disclose AI assistance, and use a human finish.
  5. **Reorders are weak:** add-ons only (second pet, ornament, bandana).

### Desk Reset Kit ($129 to $139: wool-blend felt mat + clamp-on steel cable tray + ties, clips, sleeves, magnetic holder, headphone hook, guide)

- **Why it deserves investigation:**
  - Large, durable demand: "desk mat" 33,100/mo on Google and about 20,000 on Amazon, rising year on year; "cable management" 14,800.
  - January is the peak month for this niche ("under desk cable management" and "felt desk mat" both peaked in Jan 2026), so it fits the Jan 4 test exactly.
  - You are this customer.
  - Your software edge is a Desk Fit Planner (desk size, edge profile, frame position, then the mat and tray choice). It answers the #1 review complaint, fit.
  - Natural products 2 and 3 (cable spine, second tray, swap mats) give the Ridge / Quad Lock ecosystem path.
  - The closest premium substitute, Oakywood, costs about $246 to $261 for the same pieces bought separately.
- **Economics:**
  - Base at $129 after corrections (including a possible Section 232 duty on the steel tray): target $47.87 vs the $47.93 benchmark. Fails by a hair (0.9988).
  - At $139: passes at 1.14.
  - High case: passes at 1.28. Low case: fails at 0.48.
- **What would kill it:**
  1. **No single-box US dropship path.** It needs a small inventory buy: about 25 kits at roughly $650 to $800 before freight, against a $300 working-capital line. That is an exception you must approve.
  2. **The real tariff stack** on the steel tray is unknown until a customs-broker check.
  3. **Factory felt and trays may not look premium** on the sample.
  4. **Design patents** on specific tray shapes (USD1034160S1) and a desk-mat form (USD1040227S1). Check them against the exact factory part.

### Home espresso puck-prep kit ($129, one size: 54mm Breville/Ninja)

- **Why it deserves investigation:**
  - Rising enthusiast niche (about 20k/mo across tool terms; "puck screen" +41% YoY).
  - January is the second-best month (new machines from Christmas).
  - A machine-fit quiz solves the real problem: buyers don't know their basket size.
  - Premium kits sell at $160 to $199 (Timemore, IKAPE).
- **Economics:**
  - Corrected base passes at only 1.02.
  - Low case fails at 0.49.
- **What would kill it:**
  1. Needs an inventory buy, with no US dropship path.
  2. **Patent:** US 7,992,486 covers calibrated "click" tampers until Dec 2028, so we would need a non-click design.
  3. Look-alike kits sell for $37 to $60 on Amazon.
  4. Thin margin.
- **Recommendation: park it.**

## 8. Major risks and unknowns

5. **Amboras unknowns:** pixel and conversion events, order routing to the supplier or POD, export, and API.
