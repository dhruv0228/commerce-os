Version 3 · my pick to test first

Offer first

Davie's and Young Supplements' layout: the tiers right under the photo, the best tier pre-selected, everything included shown as tiles.

  • Why it could win: it's the pattern Davie used to get his client profitable, and it puts the two-car pack (our best tier) in front of everyone.
  • Risk: it explains the product less, so it leans on the video ad doing that job.
Best for

Paid ads where the video already showed the problem. Easiest to split-test against version 1 or 2.

Honest-selling checks built in
  • No crossed-out “was” prices. The two-car pack shows its real saving against buying 2 kits at $149.
  • Door guards say “included,” not “free $40,” until we've sold them on their own.
  • No reviews until real buyers leave them. Delivery times stay a placeholder until our test order.
  • Every photo box is a shot list for the real sample. None of it goes live as shown.
